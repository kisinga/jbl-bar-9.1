Your JBL Bar, under your control.

This package automatically selects a compatible implementation. It currently includes the JBL Bar 9.1 True Wireless Surround only; Bar 800, 1000, 1300 and MK2 installation is not implemented.

Development build: full combined installation, restart and recovery qualification is still pending. The USB checks refuse unrecognized firmware and unrecognized installations.

  1. Extract the installation ZIP onto a clean FAT32 USB. Keep AutoScript and JBLToolkit at its root.
  2. Fill this form and download config.txt. Move it into the USB’s JBLToolkit folder, replacing the existing file.
  3. Safely eject, insert into the fully awake bar, and wait five minutes. Bring the USB back and read JBLToolkit/STATUS.txt and JBLToolkit/HARDWARE.txt.
  4. After CHECK_PASSED_READY_FOR_INSTALL_OR_CONFIGURE, return here, choose Install / update everything, replace the config and repeat. A blocked check needs attention; see CHECKS.txt and PREFLIGHT.txt.
  5. After installation, remove USB and open http://jbl.local on your home network. Try the bar’s IP address or port 8080 if needed. Restart the bar without USB and verify the remote and normal audio controls.

USB action Install / update detects your setup automatically. Toolkit updates keep installed settings and SSH keys. Recognized older standalone installs are migrated with their original files kept on the bar. Unknown installations stop with an explanation.

Everything here runs locally. Nothing is uploaded. Credentials are derived in your browser and saved in the config; keep the configured USB private. Keep the matching recovery ZIP on your computer. Recovery uses its own USB payload and does not need credentials.
