Good to know +

Hold + or − for repeated steps, or use your keyboard’s ↑ and ↓ keys. Press M to mute. Pending volume changes appear immediately and are confirmed by the bar; releasing a hold stops adding steps.

Power, source switching and manual Standard selection are experimental. When the bar is in standby, turn it on before adjusting sound. This page receives live updates while it’s visible. Bluetooth device names come from the bar’s pairing cache and may lag a device rename. Bluetooth status describes the audio stream; it can stay active while an app is paused. Play, pause and track controls send requests to the connected device; apps may handle them differently. Track details are not currently being reported by the tested Bluetooth connection. Night mode uses a brief mute-hold sequence, like the original remote. Cast track details appear when the receiver shares them; mirrored audio cannot report the sending app’s play/pause state. AirPlay reports an active session only.
