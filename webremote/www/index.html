Good to know +

Hold + or − for repeated steps, or use your keyboard’s ↑ and ↓ keys. Press M to mute. Pending volume changes appear immediately and are confirmed by the bar; releasing a hold stops adding steps.

Power, source switching and manual Standard selection are experimental. When the bar is in standby, turn it on before adjusting sound. This page receives live updates while it’s visible. Bluetooth device names come from the bar’s pairing cache and may lag a device rename.
