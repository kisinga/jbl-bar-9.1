Controls & tips +

Volume. Tap +/−; hold to repeat. ↑/↓ adjusts volume; M toggles mute. Clear queued taps cancels unsent changes.

Bass offset (experimental). Choose −3 to +3 dB in 0.5 dB steps, then Save offset. Applies to all five bass levels during supported Standard playback. Positive offsets may distort.

Sound. Standard disables Smart Mode. Standard and Night Mode briefly hold mute to switch. Rear levels require detached speakers.

Playback. Bluetooth controls your connected device; its stream may stay active while paused. Track details depend on the receiver. Mirrored Cast and AirPlay provide limited status.

Local control. Live updates while this page is visible. Power and source controls are experimental. Disconnecting clears pending changes; they’re never replayed.
